# Dealer Growth Platform Foundation

Pittsburgh Sauna is part of HTFO's multi-vertical proving ground for a reusable dealer growth platform.

## Rules

- Keep the Pittsburgh Sauna customer experience and SEO locally specific.
- Put dealer identity, locations, brands, products, offers, CRM settings and AI-assistant settings behind configuration/data boundaries.
- Treat sauna finder/recommendation logic as reusable platform capability fed by dealer catalog data.
- Bubbles remains the HTFO family assistant; the underlying assistant layer must be configurable for future dealers.
- Standardize lead capture and preserve campaign attribution.
- Keep manufacturer support modular so future dealers can carry different lines.
- Architectural hierarchy: Platform -> Dealer -> Site/Vertical -> Location -> Brand -> Product -> Lead.
- Prepare for future territories and CRM adapters without delaying current HTFO/Pittsburgh Sauna launches.

## Goal

A future dealer should be onboarded mainly by supplying branding, locations, territory, catalog, promotions and integrations rather than rebuilding the site from scratch.
