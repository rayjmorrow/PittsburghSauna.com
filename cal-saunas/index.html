How I’d narrow it down

Three questions usually decide it.

Question 1

Indoor or outdoor?

If it’s going outside, the Solara-Outdoor becomes the obvious Cal starting point. Indoors, move to seating capacity.

Question 2

Two, three or four?

Buy for the way you’ll use it most of the time. A couple doesn’t automatically need a four-person sauna just because guests might occasionally join.

Question 3

Compact or roomy?

The Solara-3 is a useful middle ground. The Solara-2 saves floor space; the Solara-4 buys you noticeably more interior room.
