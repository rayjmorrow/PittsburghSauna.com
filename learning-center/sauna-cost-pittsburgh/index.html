What changes the final cost?

Sauna typeMajor factorInfrared, indoor traditional and outdoor structures use different heating and construction systems.
SizeMajor factorMore seating generally means more material, larger heaters and more floor space.
HeaterTraditionalTraditional electric saunas require a heater sized to the room. Controls and stones may be separate depending on package.
ElectricalSite-specificLarger heaters generally require dedicated electrical work by a qualified electrician.
Outdoor baseOutdoor onlyA level, stable, well-drained base may add site-preparation cost before delivery.
Delivery / assemblyVariesAccess, model size and whether you assemble or use professional help can affect final project cost.

What budget should I plan?

For many Pittsburgh homeowners, a practical starting budget is roughly $4,500–$6,000 for a smaller complete infrared sauna and $5,000–$9,000+ for a traditional sauna project once the room, heater and installation variables are considered. Larger outdoor sauna projects can go higher depending on the structure and site.
