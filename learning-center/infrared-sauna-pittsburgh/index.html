Buying an infrared sauna in Pittsburgh?

Infrared is popular with homeowners who want lower air temperatures, fast startup and a simple everyday routine. Here is what matters before you choose a cabin.

Why shoppers choose infrared.

Infrared cabins use radiant emitters rather than heating stones and the whole room to traditional-sauna temperatures. That changes the feel, warm-up time and electrical setup.

Comfort

Lower air temperature

Infrared usually feels gentler than a traditional sauna because the room operates at lower temperatures while radiant heat warms the user directly.

Convenience

Fast everyday use

Many infrared cabins are ready to use sooner than traditional rooms, which makes them appealing for frequent home sessions.

Space

Compact options

One- and two-person infrared cabins can work well in basements, finished rooms and other indoor spaces where footprint matters.

What should you compare?

Cabin questions

  • How many people will actually use it most days?
  • What are the exterior dimensions?
  • What door swing and clearance do you need?
  • What wood and interior finish do you prefer?
  • Is the heater system included in the listed price?

Electrical questions

  • What voltage does the exact model require?
  • Does it need a dedicated circuit?
  • Where is the outlet or connection point?
  • Is the planned room suitable for the manufacturer’s installation requirements?
  • Will an electrician be needed before delivery?
