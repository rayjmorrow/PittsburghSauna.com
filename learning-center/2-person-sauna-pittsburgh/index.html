How much room should you allow?

Cabin dimensions are only part of the planning. You also need enough room for door swing, access, electrical connections and assembly. Measure the actual path into the room as well as the final footprint. If stairs, tight hallways or doorways are involved, tell us before delivery planning.

Infrared or traditional for two people?

Infrared is usually the simpler everyday option: lower air temperature, fast warm-up and compact footprints. Traditional is the better fit if you specifically want stones, higher heat and the option to add water for steam. Our traditional vs. infrared guide explains the experience difference.

What should a Pittsburgh buyer plan for?

Before ordering, confirm the room, electrical requirements and delivery path. Many infrared cabins have simpler electrical needs than traditional electric saunas, but the exact model determines the circuit. Traditional models typically require a dedicated hardwired heater circuit.
