Buy for normal use, not the biggest party

One of the most common sizing mistakes is buying for the largest group that might ever use the sauna. If two people will use it 90% of the time, a well-sized 3–4 person room often makes more sense than paying for and heating a much larger cabin.

Traditional is especially strong in this size range

For buyers who want classic sauna heat, stones and steam, the traditional sauna 210 and 210X are natural places to start. The main planning questions are available space, heater sizing, electrical service and whether you prefer an indoor or outdoor installation.

Read our traditional sauna Pittsburgh guide, sauna sizing guide, and installation-cost guide before deciding.

Local planning matters

A sauna that looks perfect online can still be wrong for the room. We can help verify the footprint, delivery path, electrical requirements and heater pairing before you commit. That is especially useful for basement installations and outdoor projects where access can change the plan.
