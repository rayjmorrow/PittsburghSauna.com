Traditional or Infrared?

Start with the experience—not the specs.

Choose traditional if…

You want the ritual.

Hot air, stones, steam on demand and the unmistakable feel of a classic Finnish-style sauna. Traditional heat is the right place to start when the sauna experience itself is what you’re chasing.

Choose infrared if…

You want easy daily use.

Lower-temperature radiant warmth, faster startup and an indoor setup that’s simpler to work into a normal routine. Infrared is a strong fit when convenience matters as much as the heat.
